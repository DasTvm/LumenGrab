# ADR 0011: Pinned captures

Status: accepted (M6). Designed in `LumenGrab.pen` ("M6 / Pin").

A pin keeps a picture in sight while you work in other windows: a reference, a bug, a design you copy from. It floats above every other window, has no frame and goes away when you close it.

## Decisions

- **One window per pin** (`pin-1`, `pin-2`, ...), at most **eight**; the ninth shows a notice instead of a window. Pins are not restored after a restart.
- **The picture lives in memory** (PNG bytes) and the window loads it through `lgcapture://localhost/pin-<n>`, like the overlay frames. No temporary file, no path.
- **Every command acts on the window it is called from** (`pin_zoom`, `pin_set_locked`, `pin_close`, ...). A page can only change its own pin. Nothing takes a path.
- **Sources:** a History entry (`pin_history`, by id), the card in Quick Access (`pin_quick_access`, which toggles; a second press unpins), the editor (`pin_from_png`, the flattened picture as raw bytes), and "Pin last capture" (Ctrl+Shift+P, tray) which pins the newest history entry.
- **At 100 % a pin is as big on the screen as the capture was** (device pixels divided by the scale of the screen); a picture larger than 80 % of the screen starts smaller. The wheel steps through 25, 33, 50, 67, 75, 80, 90, 100, 110, 125, 150, 175, 200, 250, 300, 400 %, around the centre of the window. A pin is never smaller than 140 logical pixels on its short side.
- **Locked = click-through** (`set_ignore_cursor_events`). A locked window cannot be clicked, so it can only be unlocked from outside: Ctrl+Shift+U or the tray's "Unlock pins" (enabled only while a pin is locked).
- **The right-click menu is native** (`window.popup_menu`) so it can reach beyond the small window; its choices are handled in Rust, the opacity choice is sent back to the page.
- **macOS:** the window sits at the floating level, joins every Space and may sit over fullscreen apps (`platform::configure_pin`); the system shadow is on. Pins do not make LumenGrab a Dock app (only History, Settings and editor windows do).
- **No format change.** A pin is never saved.
